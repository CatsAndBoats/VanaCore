At a glance

Grass
After the whole world, before the effects. The depth before it is copied first, so it casts no occlusion, takes no reflection and can be left out of the ink lines. 3.5
Glow
Mid-world, where the game lays its own avatar/trust glow; laid again over the grass once that's drawn. 2.5 3.5
Sun shadows
Casters recorded during the world; maps drawn at the start of the effects; looked up in the occlusion pass, laid on in the composite. 2.3 4.3 4.6
Scene effects
AO, bloom, god rays, fog, ink lines, reflections, DoF, FXAA: one block on the finished world, before the game's flames and spells. Phase 4
Game's effects
Flames, spells, particles: after the scene effects, so no fog or AO on them. Their light is added back later. 5.1 6.2
Water, rain
Water then rain, after the grass, both drawn over copies of the scene. 3.6 3.7
Colour grade
Last on the 3D picture: after the game's effects, on the upscaler's output when one runs. 6.3 6.4
Names over heads
Held back while the upscaler and grade run, drawn on top after: never graded or upscaled. 5.3 6.5
DLSS / FSR
At the first draw that reads the scene (the game laying it on the back buffer), after the zone lines and the effects' light. 6.3
/vana window
ImGui is its own layer, laid at window size in the very last draw, with the FPS overlay. 8.2

What the order means

  • Anything drawn before Phase 4 gets the scene effects. Grass, VanaCore's water, rain streaks, the sky and the glow all sit under the AO, shadows, fog, bloom and FXAA. Anything after it (the game's particles, late world pieces, names) does not.
  • Casters drawn after the effects can't reach this frame's maps. The game sometimes draws a third of a zone after its first particle; those pieces go into the shadow cache for the next frame (sun_late).
  • The colour grade covers the game's effects; the composite doesn't. Fog, lines and AO are in the composite (Phase 4); the grade and moonlight wait until the game has finished the 3D picture (Phase 6).
  • FXAA and DLSS/FSR don't stack. With DLSS or AMD FSR gathering frames, FXAA is skipped; FSR 1 runs on an FXAA'd scene.
  • Depth of field moves with the upscaler. Without one it's the last scene effect; with one it's laid on the upscaled output instead (a blur before it shimmered with the jitter).
  • With effects off ([graphics.effects] enabled = 0), Phase 4 is skipped except FXAA and the upscaler hand-off; the world, grass, water, rain and glow still draw.
